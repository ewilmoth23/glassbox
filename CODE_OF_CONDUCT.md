# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue with the code, not the person. Assume the other party is acting
in good faith until they clearly are not.

## What is expected

Give feedback about the work rather than the author. Accept that other people
have context you do not. When you are wrong, say so and move on. When someone
else is wrong, correct the substance without making it personal.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or personal attacks. Sexualised language or imagery.
Publishing anyone's private information. Sustained disruption of discussion.
Any of it gets the comment removed and, if repeated, the account blocked from
the repository.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any other space
associated with this project.

## Reporting

Email **ethancolewilmoth@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the project maintainer
and are not shared further without your agreement.
