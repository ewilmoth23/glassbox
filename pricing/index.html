FAQ

Where does the data come from?

26+ public OSINT sources: AIS for vessels (AISStream), ADS-B for aircraft (adsb.lol), OFAC/EU CFSP/UK OFSI consolidated sanctions, NASA FIRMS for wildfires, USGS for earthquakes, GDELT for news, EONET for natural events, NOAA SWPC for space weather, Natural Earth for boundaries. Full registry at /api/sources.

Is this just rebadged worldmonitor / globalthreatmap?

No. Both of those are AGPL — we read them for design patterns and ship 100% original code. The 9 algorithms (sanctioned-dark detection, shadow-fleet clustering, port-arrival matching, multi-juris cross-checks, etc.) are written from scratch and run on-prem. Nothing is copied.

What's the SLA?

Free tier is best-effort (currently 99.5% over the past 30 days, see live status). Team plan is 99.9% with a 1-month credit on miss. Enterprise is a contracted SLA with 4-hour response.

Can I export the data?

Free: RSS, JSON Feed, CSV (up to 5,000 rows). Pro: same plus 30-day historical CSV exports. Team: REST API, webhooks, Parquet exports for analytics, full historical (1 year). Enterprise: anything you need.

Is the AI brief actually useful or LLM filler?

It's a deterministic summary of the past hour's algorithm fires (event count + categories + top 5 entities) followed by an Ollama qwen2.5:14b-generated 3-line analyst note. Runs entirely on-prem (no OpenAI). The deterministic part is the load-bearing one; the LLM adds tone.
